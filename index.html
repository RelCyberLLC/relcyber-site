Principal

Chris Wild, Founder

Chris Wild has spent 23 years in cybersecurity, cyberspace operations, and network and systems engineering with the Department of Defense and U.S. federal agencies. The first half of his career focused on operational missions and network defense, while the second half has centered on strategy, architecture, and resilient mission support.

As a Marine Corps cyberspace officer, he led a 40-person team through multiple incident response missions that found and eradicated advanced persistent threats. His team deployed hunt capabilities across diverse mission environments and coordinated response actions under pressure.

In his civilian roles, he developed Zero Trust strategy, policy, and implementation guidance for the Army. For the Marine Corps, he led the capability gap analysis that informed funding and modernization efforts across critical mission areas.

A phased roadmap for Army-wide OT cybersecurity, with measurable annual objectives and leadership reporting.
